548 assessments delivered on time

Corpoyarumos · Python and OpenAI API

The challenge

The team needed to clear a backlog of diagnostic assessments. Each assessment informed a training plan, and each approved plan needed a curriculum.

My contribution

I automated diagnostic drafting with Python and the OpenAI API, then extended the workflow to the 548 associated plans and curriculum generation after approval.

The result

Drafting time fell from roughly 30 to 2 minutes per assessment. That helped the team deliver all 548 assessments by the deadline.

The public reconstruction uses synthetic data and runs offline. Its tests verify that only an approved plan can generate a curriculum.
